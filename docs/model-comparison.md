# Development model comparison

Run `python -m scripts.compare_models` from the repository root after Step 2. The script loads the shared development folds and the four profile fields. Each validation prediction comes from a pipeline fitted on the other four folds. The original input and optional log-income variant share assignments and seeds. The test partition stays reserved.

The mean benchmark estimates average spending from the training fold. Linear regression estimates additive differences associated with age, income, membership and city tier. A decision tree splits profiles into groups and estimates each group's mean spend. Its fixed starting settings are maximum depth 8 and minimum leaf size 20. The forest averages 50 trees with maximum depth 14 and minimum leaf size 10. Both use unscaled numeric inputs. These are fixed starting configurations for the later tree grid searches.

The ANN uses MLPRegressor with one 16-unit hidden layer to learn nonlinear combinations of profile attributes. The ANN and linear model standardise numeric inputs. The ANN also standardises the target inside the training fold and translates predictions back into spending units. Early stopping monitors internal validation R² on 10% of the outer training fold with patience 10 and an epoch cap of 150. The fold log records epochs, best internal validation score, final loss, cap status and convergence warnings. All ten ANN fits stopped below the cap after 24–53 epochs with zero convergence warnings.

Rank configurations by mean validation RMSE, which gives larger spending errors more weight. MAE expresses typical absolute error and R² measures performance relative to variation within each validation fold. Average metrics equally over the five folds. RMSE variability uses the sample standard deviation. Runtime sums fit and predict wall time for each variant over its five folds.

Original-feature linear regression led at 1,882.40 mean RMSE, followed by ANN at 1,886.94. Linear regression scored lower in all five paired folds by 1.35–11.11 units. Every learned family beat the mean benchmark of 3,754.45. Log income gave no meaningful benefit. These results describe held-out synthetic profiles under the Step 2 exchangeability assumption. The period and currency of Amount_Spent and availability of profile fields at prediction time still require confirmation.

Local results are in `reports/generated/model_comparison/`. The comparison table ranks all ten configurations. Fold scores and paired feature deltas preserve individual comparisons. Out-of-fold predictions use the development record position and fold as keys and retain observed spending for diagnostics. Versions and seed are in versions.json.
